The process

From fundus image to graded result.

i.

Upload image

Upload a retinal fundus photograph in standard JPG or PNG format.

ii.

Model analyzes

A fine-tuned EfficientNet scans the image for microaneurysms, hemorrhages, and exudates.

iii.

Severity graded

Result is classified on a 5-stage scale, from no DR to proliferative DR.

iv.

Heatmap shown

A Grad-CAM overlay highlights the retinal regions driving the prediction.

Under the hood

Built on transfer learning, read with Grad-CAM.

The classifier does the grading. Grad-CAM makes sure that grading can be checked, not just trusted.

Classifier · EfficientNet-B0

Severity grading

Pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned on labeled fundus images to grade DR across five clinical stages, evaluated with quadratic weighted kappa.

Explainability · Grad-CAM

Visual reasoning

Generates a heatmap over the retina showing which regions most influenced the prediction — hemorrhages, exudates, or microaneurysms.
