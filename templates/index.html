Target Retinal Pathologies

Trained across multi-condition fundus datasets (ODIR-5K standards)

PHYSIOLOGICAL

Normal Retina

Healthy vasculature, physiological optic disc, and intact foveal reflex.

PATHOLOGY

Cataract

Crystalline lens opacity obscuring fundus visibility and details.

NEUROPATHY

Glaucoma

Optic nerve rim thinning, increased cup-to-disc ratio, and neuroretinal loss.

MICROVASCULAR

Diabetic Retinopathy

Retinal microaneurysms, blot hemorrhages, hard exudates, and macular edema.
