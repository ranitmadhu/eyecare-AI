ABOUT EYECARE AI

Smarter screening, clearer understanding.

EyeCare AI is a final-year CSE (AI/ML) project designed to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can assist with diabetic retinopathy screening.

◉

AI-assisted screening

The system analyzes retinal fundus images and estimates diabetic retinopathy severity across five stages.

✦

Explainable results

Grad-CAM helps visualize the retinal regions that influenced the model's prediction.

✓

Screening, not diagnosis

EyeCare AI is an educational screening aid. Clinical decisions should always be made by a qualified ophthalmologist.

EYE HEALTH

Simple habits for healthier eyes.

Everyday habits can support long-term eye health. Regular professional eye examinations are also important.

01

Regular eye exams

Schedule comprehensive eye examinations as recommended by your eye-care professional.

02

Protect your eyes

Use appropriate eye protection when working around dust, chemicals, sports equipment, or other potential hazards.

03

Follow the 20-20-20 rule

During prolonged screen use, regularly look at something about 20 feet away for around 20 seconds.

04

Manage overall health

Healthy lifestyle choices and proper management of conditions such as diabetes can support eye health.
